### 💡 A simple path to your first digital product

1. **Pick a narrow problem** you or a specific audience has.
2. **Prototype fast** with an AI coding tool (Claude Code, Cursor, Bolt, v0) or a no-code builder (Bubble, Webflow, Softr).
3. **Design a simple landing page** (Carrd, Framer) and collect emails before you finish building.
4. **Set up payments** (Stripe, Lemon Squeezy, or Gumroad for something simple).
5. **Launch** on Product Hunt / Indie Hackers / a relevant community, and iterate based on real feedback.
